1. Overview & Key Principles

Dozify ("we", "our", "the App") is a GLP-1 medication tracking application developed by ErmDev ("Developer"), an individual developer based in Istanbul, Turkey. The App helps patients using GLP-1 medications (Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Mounjaro®, Saxenda®, and similar) log injections, monitor side effects, manage vials, track weight, and generate reports for their healthcare providers.

This Privacy Policy describes what information the App processes, how it is handled, and what rights you have. It applies to all versions of Dozify on iOS and Android.

Our approach to privacy rests on three principles:

  1. Data minimisation: We process only what is strictly necessary for the App to function.
  2. Local-first architecture: Health and personal data never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.
  3. Transparency: We describe every data flow in plain language — no vague catch-alls.

2.2 Purchase Verification Data

When you subscribe to Dozify Premium, the transaction is processed exclusively by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We receive only an anonymous, non-reversible subscription receipt token — we never see your payment card details, billing address, or real name. This token is validated by RevenueCat (see §4) to determine whether your subscription is active.

3. How Data Is Processed

All health data is processed locally on your device using SQLite and React Native / Expo APIs. No data is transmitted over the network during normal use. Specifically:

  • Charts and analytics are computed on-device in real time.
  • PDF reports are generated on-device using expo-print and shared only when you explicitly tap "Share" or "Save".
  • Notification scheduling runs entirely through the OS scheduler (expo-notifications) — reminder content is never sent to a server.
  • Educational articles are bundled within the app binary — no network request is made to fetch them.

The App has no backend server of its own. There is no API endpoint we operate that receives health data.

6. Push Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App schedules local notifications on your device for injection reminders and vial expiry alerts. These notifications are generated entirely on-device by the OS scheduler. No notification content or scheduling data is transmitted to any server. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device Settings.

7. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Dozify Premium is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. Subscriptions are managed through the platform (Apple / Google) and are subject to their respective store terms. We do not handle payment data. Cancellations must be made through your platform account settings.

The free tier remains fully functional indefinitely. Premium features are limited to advanced reporting (detailed reports, unlimited exports, custom date ranges).

8. Children's Privacy

Dozify is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided health data through the App, please contact us at privacy@dozify.app and we will provide guidance on deleting it from the device.

9. Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • OS-level encryption: The SQLite database benefits from iOS Data Protection and Android's encrypted storage.
  • No network transmission: The most effective protection against data breaches is never transmitting health data over the network.
  • No server-side storage: There is no database on our side that could be breached.
  • Minimal permissions: The App requests only notification permission. No camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions are requested.

We cannot guarantee absolute security of the device itself. If your device is lost or compromised, we recommend using your device's screen lock and data-wipe features.

10. Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained on your device for as long as you use the App. You have three deletion options:

  1. Selective deletion: Delete individual injection records, side effect entries, or weight logs from within the App.
  2. Full data wipe: Settings → Delete Data → "Delete All Data". This irreversibly removes all health data, settings, and vial records from the device database.
  3. App uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes all local data from the device.

Because we do not store data on any server, we hold no data to delete on our end. Deletion is instantaneous and complete.
