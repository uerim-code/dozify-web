How it works

Three steps. No setup headache.

  1. Add your medication

    Pick a weekly or daily injection — Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Mounjaro®, Zepbound®, Trulicity®, Saxenda®, Victoza® — or a daily tablet: Wegovy® pill, Ozempic® tablet, Rybelsus® or Foundayo™. Set your dose and schedule.

  2. Log every injection

    Tap to record the dose, time, and injection site on a body diagram. Smart rotation suggests your next site.

  3. Share with your doctor

    Export a PDF report with up to the last 180 days of treatment history, weight progress, and side-effect log: perfect for your next visit.

Dozify never uploads your records

No account, no in-app ads, no profile. Your injections, weight and measurements are stored on your device. Delete the app and the records on this phone go with it. Files you exported, anything you wrote to Apple Health and your own iPhone backups are separate.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dozify free?

Yes. Dose logging and dose reminders are free, with no time limit. Also free: one active medication, site rotation, weight, water and side-effect logging, lab values, victories, check-ins, your full history, the summary PDF (2 a month), Classic and Dolphin themes, the 26 guides and your first AI meal-photo analysis. Premium: AI meal-photo analyses after the first one, a second (and further) active medication, the estimated medication level curve, all trend charts and the side-effect timing view, weight trend and time-to-goal projection, body measurements and composition, nutrition tracking, your doctor's dose plan, milestones and streaks, weekly summary, appointment mode, dose-change notes, four more themes, the smart water reminder, advanced reminders (refill, vial expiry, dose step-up day), detailed and doctor PDF reports, vial management, and adding new progress photos. Subscription and cancellation details are on the support page.

Is my data private?

Your health records (doses, weight, side effects, progress photos) are stored on your device and never uploaded. The one exception is a meal photo you choose to analyse with AI calories: it is sent, after you agree, through our server to Google Gemini and is not stored by the app. There is no account and no cloud sync, and the app shows no advertising. Usage statistics tied to a random app ID, not your name. They record that a feature was used, never a medication, dose, weight or symptom. Dozify also measures whether an install came from one of its own App Store or Meta campaigns; that measurement runs behind Apple's tracking permission and never includes your health records. Delete the app and the records on this phone go with it. Files you exported, anything you wrote to Apple Health and your own iPhone backups are separate.

Which medications does Dozify support?

Dozify helps you track popular GLP-1 medications, including Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Mounjaro®, Zepbound®, Saxenda®, Trulicity®, and Victoza®, plus daily tablets: the Wegovy® pill, Ozempic® tablet, Rybelsus® and Foundayo™. These brand names are trademarks of their respective owners, and Dozify is not affiliated with these companies.

Does Dozify work without internet?

Yes for tracking and reminders. AI meal photos and buying Premium need a connection. You never need to sign in or sync to use it.

Can Dozify remind me of my injection?

Yes. You can set a weekly or daily reminder that matches your medication's schedule, so it is easier to stay consistent with your routine.

Does Dozify replace my doctor?

No. Dozify is an educational tracking tool, not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice. Always follow your doctor and your prescription — what the app refuses to do is written out in full.

Can I track weight and side effects?

Yes. You can record your weight and a side-effect diary for free. Body measurements, trend charts, a weight projection and a time-to-goal estimate based on your own data are part of Premium.

Can I bring my data from another GLP-1 app?

Yes, and it is free. If the app you are leaving offers an export, export from it, open Settings → Import in Dozify and pick the file. It is built to read Shotsy, GLPzy and GlucoPal exports from their published formats, and any CSV with a date column and a weight, dose or symptom column; if you can get a CSV of your Glapp data, Dozify can read it. Not every format has been tested with a real export. It shows you what it found (how many injections, weights and side effects) before writing anything, skips records you already have, and backs up your existing data first. More on the switching page.

Is Dozify available on Android?

Not yet. Dozify is iPhone only for now, on the App Store.
