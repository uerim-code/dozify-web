Looking for a MeAgain alternative? How Dozify differs, and when to stay

MeAgain is the most-rated GLP-1 tracker on the US App Store. If it is not the right fit for you, here is how Dozify compares, using MeAgain's own listing, and where MeAgain is the better choice. We make Dozify, so weigh that.

When MeAgain is the better pick

  • You use an Android phone, or switch between Android and iPhone. Dozify is iPhone only.
  • Food is the centre of your tracking: barcode scans, food search, fiber and water goals next to your doses.
  • You want an AI companion you can message, including over iMessage.
  • You want an app with tens of thousands of ratings to read before you install. Dozify has none in the US yet.

Where Dozify is different

  • Dose logging and dose reminders are free, with no time limit. One active medication, injection-site rotation, weight, water and side effects are free too.
  • No account. You open the app and start; there is nothing to sign up for.
  • 22 languages, including the AI meal estimate, which answers in the language you use.
  • Reminders that work with the app closed. "I took it" on the notification records the dose.
  • The level curve is labelled an estimate, and for medicines taken by mouth it is not drawn at all rather than guessed.

Bringing your history

Dozify reads Shotsy, GLPzy and GlucoPal exports and any CSV file with a date column and a weight, dose or symptom column. MeAgain's listing does not mention an export, so check inside MeAgain whether you can save your data as a file. If you can get a CSV, Dozify shows how many records it found before anything is written and backs up first. Questions

Is Dozify free?

It is free to download, and dose logging and dose reminders are free with no time limit. Premium adds things like the estimated medication-level curve, AI meal estimates after the first one and the doctor report; on the US App Store it is $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

Is there a Dozify app for Android?

No. Dozify is iPhone only. If you need Android, MeAgain has an Android version.

Can Dozify import my MeAgain data?

Only if you can get it as a CSV file with a date column and a weight, dose or symptom column. Dozify does not have a dedicated MeAgain importer, and MeAgain's listing does not mention an export.

Does Dozify tell me what dose to take?

No. It records the plan your prescriber gives you and never proposes one.
