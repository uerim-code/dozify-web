Coming from GlucoPal? Bring the history with you

Export from GlucoPal, open it in Dozify, and see every record that is about to be written before one of them is. Free, and the file never leaves your phone.

Why the history is the hard part

Months of injections, weigh-ins and side effects are the reason people stay in an app they have stopped liking. Nobody retypes a year of shots, so the record becomes the lock. Moving it across is the only part that matters.

The columns Dozify looks for in a GlucoPal export

Open your exported file and compare the header row. Dozify matches a column by any of these names, and it does not mind capitalisation or a unit written in brackets. Anything it cannot place is listed on the preview screen by name, so you can see what was left out rather than wondering.

Nothing is written until you say so

The preview shows how many weights, injections and side effects were found, the dates they span, how many are already here, and how many rows could not be read and why. Dozify writes an automatic backup to your device first, and the import itself is one transaction — if anything fails it is rolled back completely, because half an imported history is worse than none.

If your dose column is headed “units”

Those rows are listed for you instead of being converted. A unit is a volume on a syringe, and how many milligrams sit in it depends on the concentration of your vial — a number the file does not carry. Writing them into a milligram field would record the wrong dose without anything downstream questioning it, so Dozify shows them and lets you enter the amount your prescriber uses. What comes across, and what does not

Imported: weight entries, injections with date, time, dose, site and notes, and side effects with their severity.

Not imported: vials, progress photos and check-ins — either there is no equivalent or the mapping would be a guess, and a guessed record is worse than an absent one.

Add a medication first if you want injections. A logged shot has to belong to one. If you have not set one up, the preview says so and brings in weights and side effects only; add the medication and import the same file again, and nothing already here is added twice.

If the file is not recognised

Dozify says so rather than importing zero rows quietly. Any CSV with a date column will read. If yours does not, send it from Settings → Suggest a feature and the format gets added — a file we cannot read is our gap, not yours.
