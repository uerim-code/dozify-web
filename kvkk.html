2. Categories of Personal Data & Legal Basis

Dozify processes the following categories of personal data, all of which are stored exclusively on the user's own device and never transmitted to the data controller or any third party:

CategorySpecific DataKVKK ClassificationLegal Basis (KVKK Art. 5–6)
Health & Medication Data Injection records (date, dose, site), side effect diary, weight entries, medication type and vial details Special category personal data (sensitive) — Art. 6 Explicit consent of the data subject (Art. 6/2)
Subscription Status Anonymous subscription receipt token (no identity information) General personal data — Art. 5 Performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c)
App Preferences Language, notification preferences, unit settings General personal data — Art. 5 Legitimate interest of the data controller (Art. 5/2-f)

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Enabling the user to log and review GLP-1 injection records and side effects.
  • Generating visual analytics and PDF reports for personal use and healthcare provider consultations.
  • Managing vial lifecycle (open date, expiry, storage events).
  • Delivering scheduled push notifications for injection reminders and vial expiry alerts.
  • Verifying active premium subscription status to unlock advanced features.

Data is not used for profiling, advertising, research, or any purpose other than those listed above.

4. Technical Architecture & Non-Transfer Declaration

Dozify is architected as a fully offline, local-first application. All health and preference data is stored in a SQLite database residing on the user's device. The application has no backend server and makes no API calls that transmit health data.

As a consequence: the data controller (ErmDev) never receives, stores, or has access to the health data entered by users. This is a technical impossibility by design, not merely a policy commitment.

The only outbound network communication is:

  • RevenueCat SDK — transmits an anonymous app installation ID and subscription receipt for validation. No health data is included.
  • Platform IAP APIs (Apple / Google) — used only during subscription purchase/restore flows.

5. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Health data is not transferred to any domestic or foreign server. The anonymous subscription token processed by RevenueCat (USA) and Apple/Google is not personal data under KVKK as it contains no directly or indirectly identifying health information. Nevertheless, RevenueCat operates under standard contractual clauses and maintains a GDPR Data Processing Agreement available at revenuecat.com/dpa.

7. Your Rights under KVKK (Article 11)

①

Right to be Informed

Right to learn whether your personal data is being processed.

②

Right of Access

Right to access your data and obtain information about processing purposes.

③

Right to Rectification

Right to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.

④

Right to Erasure

Exercise directly in-app: Settings → Delete Data. For subscription data, contact us.

⑤

Right to Object

Right to object to processing and request restriction of processing.

⑥

Right to Data Portability

Exercise directly: Settings → Export Data (CSV format).

⑦

Right to Compensation

Right to claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing.

⑧

Right to Complain to KVKK Board

If your request is unanswered within 30 days, you may apply to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

To exercise your rights, send a written request to privacy@dozify.app with the subject "KVKK Request". We respond within 30 calendar days. Identity verification may be required.

8. Consent & Withdrawal

By downloading and using Dozify, you acknowledge this notice and provide explicit consent for your health data to be processed locally on your device for the purposes described above. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting all data via Settings → Delete Data and uninstalling the application. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

Teknik Yapı ve Aktarım Yokluğu Beyanı

Dozify tamamen çevrimdışı ve yerel-önce mimariye sahiptir. Girdiğiniz tüm sağlık verileri yalnızca kendi cihazınızda SQLite veri tabanında saklanır. Uygulama, sağlık verisi içeren hiçbir API çağrısı yapmamaktadır. Veri sorumlusu ErmDev, kullanıcı sağlık verilerine teknik olarak erişememektedir.

Yurt İçi / Yurt Dışı Aktarım

Sağlık verileri yurt içi veya yurt dışı hiçbir sunucuya aktarılmamaktadır. Abonelik doğrulaması için RevenueCat (ABD) üzerinden yalnızca anonim cihaz kimliği geçmektedir; bu veri KVKK kapsamında kişisel veri niteliği taşımamaktadır.

Saklama Süreleri

Sağlık verileri, kullanıcı silene veya uygulamayı kaldırana kadar cihazda saklanır. Sunucuda tutulan veri bulunmamaktadır.

KVKK Madde 11 Kapsamındaki Haklarınız

Bilgi edinme, erişim, düzeltme, silme, aktarım kısıtlama, itiraz, taşınabilirlik ve zarar tazminini talep etme haklarına sahipsiniz. Uygulama içindeki "Verileri Sil" ve "Veri Dışa Aktar" özellikleri bu hakların büyük bölümünü doğrudan karşılamaktadır.

Yazılı başvurular: privacy@dozify.app (konu: "KVKK Talebi") — 30 gün içinde yanıt verilir. Yetersiz yanıt durumunda Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (kvkk.gov.tr)'na başvurabilirsiniz.

Açık Rıza ve Geri Alma

Uygulamayı indirip kullanarak bu metni okuduğunuzu ve sağlık verilerinizin yukarıda belirtilen amaçlarla cihazınızda işlenmesine açık rıza verdiğinizi kabul edersiniz. Rızanızı her zaman Ayarlar → Verileri Sil aracılığıyla geri alabilirsiniz.
